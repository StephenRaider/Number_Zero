# Highway asset attribution

The following models were supplied by the user as GLBs. Attribution and source
information are retained from their embedded Sketchfab metadata. All are
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- **Low road**, by [Yarelon](https://sketchfab.com/yarelon111):
  [source model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-road-e353f90e42b542689bade5e3f714f710).
  Adapted road textures, cropped/repeated to two two-lane carriageways; resized.
- **Road pack (not including the road)**, by [drcrazzie](https://sketchfab.com/drcrazzie):
  [source model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/road-pack-not-including-the-road-021707f910d04907ab576c856a749628).
  Extracted street lamps and guardrails; normalized scale/orientation, buried
  mounting pegs clipped; rendered as separate isometric colour/normal/depth/emission layers.
- **Highway Sign**, by [haleem](https://sketchfab.com/haleem4):
  [source model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/highway-sign-28c2081eb3664308a53d38c674e08eff).
  Extracted gantry/sign assembly; normalized scale/orientation and rendered as
  separate isometric colour/normal/depth layers. English faces replaced with
  fictional Japanese destinations; sign UVs adapted for readable mirrored-view
  lettering. Original floor excluded. Japanese sign-face artwork is generated
  for Number Zero; no font files are redistributed.

Original models, material images, Blender scenes and intermediate renders stay
in the experiment workspace; only runtime exports are included here.
