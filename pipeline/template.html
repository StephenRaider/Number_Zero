Testarossa Relight Test

Isometric sprite rendered from the 3D model with flat colour, normal, depth and specular maps. Move the light in the grid space and check whether the car takes light the way a car should. Orthographic 2:1 pixel isometric: 30° elevation, 45° azimuth, so a 1 m ground tile is 28×14 px at the 128 px scale. Glass is tinted black.

Drag on the stage to move the light across the ground at the current height. Left and right views are mirrors, so the mirror switch gives the other two headings from the same maps.
